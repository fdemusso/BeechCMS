BeechCMS uses a layered licensing model to ensure the project remains sustainable while providing maximum freedom for self-hosting and general use.

## Business Source License 1.1 (BSL) Explained

The `apps/api` and `apps/dashboard` components are licensed under the Business Source License 1.1 (BSL 1.1).

### What does this mean?

The BSL is a source-available license that allows free use for almost all purposes, **except** offering BeechCMS as a managed SaaS service (e.g., a "CMS-as-a-Service") to third parties in direct competition with the official BeechCMS Cloud.

- **Self-hosting is always free.** You can host BeechCMS for your own company, your personal projects, or your own internal use.
- **Agency use is permitted.** You can use BeechCMS to build websites for your clients.
- **You can fork and modify.** As long as you don't violate the competitive use restriction.

### The "Change Date"

The BSL is temporary. On the **Change Date** (which is 2030-05-26 for the current version), the license for these components automatically converts to the **GNU General Public License v2.0 or later (GPLv2+)**, which is a fully Open Source Initiative (OSI) approved license. 
Every new release will have its own 4-year rolling Change Date.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I self-host BeechCMS for my own projects? 
**YES**. Self-hosting for internal, personal, or corporate projects is fully permitted.

### Can I use BeechCMS for my clients' websites? 
**YES**. Agencies and freelancers can build and deliver websites using BeechCMS.

### Can I sell websites built with BeechCMS? 
**YES**. 

### Can I fork and modify BeechCMS? 
**YES**, as long as your modifications and use do not violate the Additional Use Grant (i.e., you don't create a competing managed CMS service).

### Can I launch a SaaS CMS product powered by BeechCMS? 
**NO**. This requires a commercial license. Offering a hosted version of BeechCMS as a service to third parties is restricted.

### When does the code become fully open source? 
The code converts to GPL v2.0 or later on **2030-05-26** (the "Change Date").
